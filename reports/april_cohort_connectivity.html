Why the number is "78%"

The org's headline April GTG ≈ 78% is the connected cohort: sellers reached within 3 days convert at 77.6% (day-0 arrivals at 78.8%). The blended 75.5% is lower only because it carries the small unreachable tail — the ~8% who connected late or never, converting near 50%. Both are right; they differ only in denominator. Quote 78% and you are quoting the reachable seller.

What the cohort is telling us

Connectivity was a solved problem in April

91.7% connected within 3 days, 80.6% within a single day. The calling operation was firing on all cylinders — a different regime from July's ~60%.

Within 3 days, speed barely matters — the cliff is after day 3

Day 0→3 is nearly flat (78.8 → 67.6%). The real drop is connect-after-3-days (58.7%) and RNR (26.9%). The lever isn't "d0 vs d1" — it's "don't let anyone slip past day 3 or go dark."

RNR is the loss pocket — small but brutal

Just 26 sellers, but they convert at 27%, a third of the cohort rate. Dialed and ghosted — active disengagement, not an ops gap. This is where the recoverable-vs-lost question lives.

First touch lands where the funnel says it should

94% of first connects happen on the CAGD (doc-collection) task — the funnel's true entry point. Zero inbound calls: every connect is proactive outbound reach.

The value of connectivity depends on the regime

April is saturated (92%), so pushing connectivity harder has little headroom — GTG is decided further down the funnel. July (60%) has real room, so it's a live lever there. Caveat: July's steeper 2.82× is partly immaturity — a clean comparison needs GTG pinned to the same fixed horizon on both cohorts.
